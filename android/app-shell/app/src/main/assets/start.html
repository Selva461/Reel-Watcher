Step 1 of 2

Install Termux

Your Termux is from the Play Store

That version is old and limited, and Reel Shelf cannot use it. Uninstall it first (long-press the Termux icon, Uninstall), then tap the button below.

Termux is a free app that runs Reel Shelf on your phone. Get it from F-Droid (not the Play Store).

On the F-Droid page, tap Download APK, open it, and tap Install. Then come back here.

Step 2 of 2

Set up Reel Shelf (once)

Takes about 15 minutes and downloads about 1 GB, so use Wi-Fi and keep the phone charging.

  1. In Termux, long-press the black screen and tap Paste.
  2. Tap Enter on the keyboard.
  3. If Android asks for storage access, tap Allow.
  4. Come back here to watch the progress. Reel Shelf opens by itself when it is ready.
